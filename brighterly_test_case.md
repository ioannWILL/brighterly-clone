Product Manager Test Case:

Brighterly — From Tutoring to Learning Platform



This is a test / consulting project. Our goal is to expand Brighterly learning platform. Right now, users get 90% of educational value out of one-on-one lessons. Our goal for 2026 is to release platform features that move the educational value out of the lesson to the platform itself, mostly by the means of AI tools and self-learning experiences.

# Brighterly: Current Status

Brighterly is an online math and English learning school for children in grades 1–9 - curated marketplace

The current core model is 1:1 online tutoring

Primary market: United States

Core value today:

personalized lessons

live interaction with a tutor

strong focus on school performance and learning outcomes

# The Business Goal

Move from a tutoring product to a platform that creates value outside live tutor-led sessions.

We plan to achieve this by creating a self-learning section of our platform, that will allow students to learn “outside” of weekly tutoring sessions. New feature impact: at least 30% of active users using it.

Constraints:

Tutoring remains a core part of the product

The new platform should:

complement tutoring

deliver standalone value

have the potential to scale without proportional growth in supply or support teams, relying primarily on product-driven growth
